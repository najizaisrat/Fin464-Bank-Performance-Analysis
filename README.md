# Comparative Analysis of Three Bangladeshi Banks
 
A FIN464 (Bank Management, Section 04) group report from North South University comparing **NCC Bank, Mercantile Bank and Agrani Bank PLC** using their 2024 financial statements.
 
## Summary
 
The banks are compared on liquidity, financial risk, profitability, capital adequacy (Basel I compliance), reserves and provisioning, and investor perception.
 
- **NCC Bank:** strongest overall, with the best profitability, capital adequacy and leverage, and a positive market response.
- **Mercantile Bank:** average, with adequate capital but provisioning shortfalls and withheld dividends.
- **Agrani Bank:** weakest, with negative profitability, high leverage and failure to meet Basel I capital requirements.
The report ends with recommendations for the weakest and best-performing banks.
